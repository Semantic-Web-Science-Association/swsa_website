<strong>The Semantic Web Science Association (SWSA) is a non-profit organisation for promotion and exchange of the scholarly work in Semantic Web and related fields throughout the world</strong>. The main SWSA activities include:

- supervision of the organisation of the **[International Semantic Web Conference series (ISWC)]({{ "/conferences/#international-semantic-web-conference-iswc" | relative_url }})**; see **[Call for Bids](/host-iswc/)** for further details.
- supervision of the organisation of the **[Extended Semantic Web Conference series (ESWC)](https://eswc-conferences.org/)**;
- the organisation and support of workshops, tutorials, or summer schools in the field of the Semantic Web;
- fostering excellence in the field through a number of **[awards]({{ "/awards/" | relative_url }})**;
- co-operation with scientific journals related to the Semantic Web.
