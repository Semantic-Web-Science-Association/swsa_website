<strong>Applications for 2028 have closed. The call to host ESWC2029 will be open early 2027 at the latest.</strong>

The Semantic Web Science Association (<a href="{{ '/' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>SWSA</strong></a>) is seeking statements of interest from organizations or consortia interested in hosting the 25th Extended Semantic Web Conference, ESWC 2028. The conference will take place in Europe in the second half of May or first week of June 2028.

Organizations wishing to host it should contact <strong>Prof John Domingue</strong> at <a href="mailto:john.domingue@open.ac.uk?subject=Hosting%20ESWC%202028">john.domingue@open.ac.uk</a>, who will work with the SWSA members to coordinate the bidding process. Any organization may apply to host the conference, but the local organizing committee <strong>must</strong> include a representative of a local research group with a track record in semantic technologies.

The bidding process consists of two stages. During the first stage, SWSA solicits the statements of interest through an open call. The expressions of interest should be submitted using the <a href="{{ '/assets/files/SWSA-ESWC-SoI v 1.0_0.pdf' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>statement of interest form</strong></a>.

Once the first phase is complete, SWSA will <strong>shortlist</strong> a number of applications, which will be invited to submit a full proposal, using the <a href="{{ '/assets/files/SWSA-ESWC-Application_2026 v 1.0_0.pdf' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>ESWC 2028 full application form</strong></a> and <a href="{{ '/assets/files/ESWC Budget Proposal Template v 1.0_2.xlsx' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>budget template</strong></a>. The shortlisted applications will be expected to make a presentation (remotely) during the bi-annual SWSA meeting, which is due to be held in June 2026. SWSA will make the decision on the 2028 host at that meeting.

More information about the ESWC Conference Series and the bidding process can be found in the <a href="{{ '/assets/files/SWSA-ESWC-Hosting-Guide v 1.0_0.pdf' | relative_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>ESWC Hosting Guide</strong></a>.

The important dates for applying to host ESWC2028 are:

- April 20th, 2026: Deadline for receiving statements of interest.
- April 30th, 2026: Notifications to shortlisted bids are sent out.
- May 28th, 2026: Formal applications received from shortlisted bids.
- June 2026: Presentation of the bid at the virtual SWSA meeting. SWSA decides on the location for the 2028 conference.
